01 The short version

We collect the information you send us, such as your email and project brief, use it to respond and deliver requested freelance project work, never sell it, and delete it when it is no longer needed. That is the whole thing. Everything below is just the detail.

02 Who we are

AzmeerPirani offers freelance video editing, graphic design, and embedded electronics services. When this policy says we, us, or the studio, that is who we mean. You can reach us any time at azmeerpirani3@gmail.com.

03 What we collect

Two kinds. Things you hand us: your name, email, company, links, and whatever you write in the contact form or an email, so we can reply. And things gathered automatically when you visit: basic, privacy-respecting analytics like page views and country-level location, plus standard server logs.

04 How we use it

To reply to your message, plan and deliver the freelance work you have asked for, keep the site running, and understand which information is useful. That is it. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not enjoy spam any more than you do.

05 Cookies and analytics

We keep cookies to a minimum. Any analytics we run are aggregate and are not used to follow you around the web. If we ever add something that needs your consent, we will ask first, clearly.

06 Who we share it with

Only the handful of tools we use to operate, for example our email provider, our scheduling tool, and our host. They process data on our behalf under their own terms. We never sell your data, and we never share it for advertising.

07 How long we keep it

Enquiries and project messages are kept while we are talking and for a reasonable period after, in case the conversation picks back up. Ask us to delete it and we will, unless we are legally required to hold on to it.

08 Your choices and rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email azmeerpirani3@gmail.com and we will sort it out, usually within a few days.

09 Security

We use reputable providers and sensible practices to protect the little we hold. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat your information the way we would want ours treated.

10 Where your data lives

We are a remote studio based in India, working with clients worldwide. Your information may be processed in other countries by the providers above. We only use providers we trust to handle it responsibly.

11 Children

Our freelance services are intended for creators, businesses, and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

12 Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be obvious, not buried in a paragraph.

13 Talk to us

Questions about any of this? Email azmeerpirani3@gmail.com and a real person will answer.
